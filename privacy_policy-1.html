Idle Ring — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026  ·  Developer: Idle Ring Entertainment (Michael Sostaric)  ·  Contact: idleringentertainment@gmail.com

Not a substitute for legal advice. This policy describes the app's actual data practices in plain language, for Google Play's Data Safety requirements and as the public privacy policy for Idle Ring. If you have specific legal concerns about your region, we'd recommend having a lawyer review it, but it reflects exactly what the app does today.

What this app is

Idle Ring is a single-player idle/incremental action RPG for Android. Optional online features (cloud save, account linking across devices, leaderboards, clans) are available for players who choose to use them. Everything described below only applies if and when you use those online features — the game can be played fully offline without creating an online account.

Information we collect

WhatDetailsWhy we collect it
Device/account identifier A randomly generated identifier created on your device when you first use an online feature. This is not your name, email, or phone number. To recognise your account across sessions and keep your save, scores and clan membership tied to you.
Display name The name you choose to show other players. Shown publicly on leaderboards and in clans so other players can identify you.
Clan name, tag and messages If you create or join a clan: the clan's name, tag, and its "message of the day" if one is set. To let players form and manage clans together.
Game progress and cloud save Your character's save data (level, items, skills, currencies, settings) when cloud sync is turned on. So your progress isn't lost if you change devices or reinstall, and so it can sync between your own devices.
Linked devices If you link a second device to your account using a one-time code, that device's own randomly generated identifier is added to your account. Codes themselves are not stored in readable form, only a short-lived scrambled version that expires after 10 minutes and can only be used once. To let you play the same account across more than one device, without needing an email or password.
Gameplay results Dungeon clear times, boss damage scores, and the build (gear, skills, runes, world tier) used to achieve them. To power leaderboards. Build details are shown publicly next to leaderboard entries so other players can see how a result was achieved.
Technical and log data IP address and request timestamps, collected automatically by our hosting providers (PlayFab and Microsoft Azure) as part of normal server operation. A short-lived, scrambled (salted hash) version of your IP address is also used for one day to limit abuse of account registration. Security, abuse prevention, and diagnosing problems with the service.

We do not collect your real name, email address, physical address, or phone number through these online features. The app does not currently support purchases, so no payment information is collected.

How your information is shared

Your gameplay data is processed by two services acting on our behalf, both operated by Microsoft:

  • PlayFab — handles account login, identity, and account linking.
  • Microsoft Azure — runs the server logic and stores game data (saves, leaderboards, clans).

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers. Your display name, clan tag, and build details tied to leaderboard entries are visible to other players of the app, by design — that is the leaderboard feature. We do not otherwise share your data with other companies except as required by law.

Data retention

Your data is kept for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account (see below), your account data, cloud saves, leaderboard entries, shared build information, linked devices, and clan membership are removed from our servers. Game progress saved only on your device is not affected by an online account deletion — you can keep playing offline.

Your choices and rights

  • Play offline. Online features are optional. You can play the entire game without creating an online account.
  • Delete your account. You can delete your online account and its associated data at any time from within the app (Options → Online → Delete online account), or by emailing idleringentertainment@gmail.com. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.
  • Turn off cloud sync. You can turn cloud sync off at any time; your existing cloud save is not deleted automatically when you do this.
  • Account recovery note. If you lose access to every device linked to your account and have no unused link code, your online account cannot be recovered. Your characters and progress on each device are unaffected either way.

Children's privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13 (or the relevant minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Data is transmitted using HTTPS encryption. We take reasonable steps to protect your information, but no online service can guarantee complete security.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app's online features change. Significant changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
