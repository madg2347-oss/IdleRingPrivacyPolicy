Idle Ring — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026  ·  Developer: Idle Ring Entertainment (Michael Sostaric)  ·  Contact: idleringentertainment@gmail.com

Not a substitute for legal advice. This policy describes the app's actual data practices in plain language, for Google Play's Data Safety requirements and as the public privacy policy for Idle Ring. If you have specific legal concerns about your region, we'd recommend having a lawyer review it, but it reflects exactly what the app does today.

What this app is

Idle Ring is a single-player idle/incremental action RPG for Android. Optional online features (cloud save, account linking across devices, leaderboards, clans) are available for players who choose to use them. Everything described below only applies if and when you use those online features — the game can be played fully offline without creating an online account.

Information we collect

WhatDetailsWhy we collect it
Device/account identifier A randomly generated identifier created on your device when you first use an online feature. This is not your name, email, or phone number. To recognise your account across sessions and keep your save, scores and clan membership tied to you.
Display name The name you choose to show other players. Shown publicly on leaderboards and in clans so other players can identify you.
Clan name, tag and messages If you create or join a clan: the clan's name, tag, and its "message of the day" if one is set. To let players form and manage clans together.
Game progress and cloud save Your character's save data (level, items, skills, currencies, settings) when cloud sync is turned on. So your progress isn't lost if you change devices or reinstall, and so it can sync between your own devices.
Linked devices If you link a second device to your account using a one-time code, that device's own randomly generated identifier is added to your account. Codes themselves are not stored in readable form, only a short-lived scrambled version that expires after 10 minutes and can only be used once. To let you play the same account across more than one device, without needing an email or password.
Gameplay results Dungeon clear times, boss damage scores, and the build (gear, skills, runes, world tier) used to achieve them. To power leaderboards. Build details are shown publicly next to leaderboard entries so other players can see how a result was achieved.
Linked accounts If you create extra accounts (up to five per player), our server keeps a random family number, the list of your accounts and when each was created. Other players can see which accounts belong together: on clan rosters, leaderboards and profiles an account can show the display names of your other accounts. Only display names are shown. The game tells you this and asks you to confirm before your first extra account is created. To link your accounts, enforce the five-account limit, and apply bans to all of a player's accounts.
Shared Bank Items and currency you deposit into the Bank shared by your linked accounts, which account deposited each one, and a record of deposits and withdrawals. So you can move items between your own accounts, and so a crash or an older save cannot duplicate or lose them.
Blocked players If you block a player, our server keeps the list of accounts you have blocked and when you blocked each one (up to 200). A block covers all of your linked accounts and all of the blocked player's linked accounts. The blocked player is not told. To hide blocked players from you on leaderboards, clan rosters, the clan feed and profiles, and to stop them joining a clan where you are the leader or an officer.
Friends Your friend code (a random code you can share; you can replace it at any time), the friend requests you send and receive, and your friends list. Friends see each other's display name and the day each was last online. So you can add friends by code and see them in the game, and so friends can message you in chat.
Chat The messages you send in clan chat, global chat and direct messages; your chat settings (who may message you); and the age range you choose before your first chat (under 13, 13 to 17, or 18 and over). Your age range is not verified and applies to all your linked accounts. To deliver messages, to keep chat safe (people under 13 cannot chat; people aged 13 to 17 can only exchange direct messages with friends), and to review reports.
Technical and log data IP address and request timestamps, collected automatically by our hosting providers (PlayFab and Microsoft Azure) as part of normal server operation. A short-lived, scrambled (salted hash) version of your IP address is also used for one day to limit abuse of account registration. Security, abuse prevention, and diagnosing problems with the service.

We do not collect your real name, email address, physical address, or phone number through these online features. The app does not currently support purchases, so no payment information is collected.

How your information is shared

Your gameplay data is processed by two services acting on our behalf, both operated by Microsoft:

  • PlayFab — handles account login, identity, and account linking.
  • Microsoft Azure — runs the server logic and stores game data (saves, leaderboards, clans, chat).
  • Azure AI Content Safety (Microsoft) — automatically checks chat messages for hate, sexual content, violence and self-harm before other players see them. Messages that fail the check are not shown.

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers. Your display name, clan tag, and build details tied to leaderboard entries are visible to other players of the app, by design — that is the leaderboard feature. If you use linked accounts, other players can also see which display names belong to the same player. We do not otherwise share your data with other companies except as required by law.

Data retention

Your data is kept for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account (see below), your account data, cloud saves, leaderboard entries, shared build information, linked devices, and clan membership are removed from our servers, and the account is removed from its linked-accounts family. Deleting the last account of a family also deletes its shared Bank and everything in it. Your list of blocked players is deleted with your account. Clan and global chat messages are kept for 7 days and direct messages for 30 days, then deleted; deleting your account deletes your direct-message threads and your friends list. Messages you already sent stay visible, under your display name, to the people who could already see them until their normal retention period ends (7 days for clan and global chat, 30 days for direct messages), so they can still be reported, and are then deleted. Reported messages are an exception: when a message is reported, a copy of it and the 10 messages before and after it is kept for 90 days (365 days for threats, self-harm and child-safety reports), even if the accounts involved are deleted, so the report can be reviewed and acted on. Game progress saved only on your device is not affected by an online account deletion — you can keep playing offline.

Your choices and rights

  • Play offline. Online features are optional. You can play the entire game without creating an online account.
  • Delete your account. You can delete your online account and its associated data at any time from within the app (Options or Social → Delete online account; "Delete all my accounts" removes every linked account at once), or by emailing idleringentertainment@gmail.com. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.
  • Block a player. You can block or unblock a player at any time in the game. Blocking is private: the other player is not told.
  • Chat settings. You can choose who may send you direct messages: friends and clanmates (the default), anyone (18 and over only), or nobody. If you or the other player are 13 to 17, direct messages are only possible between friends. Chat can be used only after you choose your age range and accept the chat rules.
  • Report a player. You can report a player, or a single chat message, from the game. A reviewer reads only messages that were reported. We review reports within 48 hours and act on those that break the rules, up to removing names or banning accounts.
  • Linked accounts and bans. Extra accounts are optional. If one of your linked accounts is banned for breaking the rules (for example cheating), your other linked accounts are banned too.
  • Turn off cloud sync. You can turn cloud sync off at any time; your existing cloud save is not deleted automatically when you do this.
  • Account recovery note. If you lose access to every device linked to your account and have no unused link code, your online account cannot be recovered. Your characters and progress on each device are unaffected either way.

Children's privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13 (or the relevant minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Data is transmitted using HTTPS encryption. We take reasonable steps to protect your information, but no online service can guarantee complete security.
